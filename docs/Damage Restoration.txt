Damage Restoration
==================

把 MacPower 拖进「应用程序」后，打开「终端」，复制下面这一行并回车（会要一次密码）：

sudo xattr -rd com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacPower.app

不要把这个 .txt 改成 .sh 再双击。从网上下载的文件带着隔离属性，改后缀去不掉，系统照样会拦截。

---

Drag MacPower into Applications, open Terminal, paste this line, and press Return (it asks for your password):

sudo xattr -rd com.apple.quarantine /Applications/MacPower.app

Do not rename this .txt to .sh and double-click it. Quarantine stays on the downloaded file; renaming does not remove it.
